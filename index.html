ABOUT ME

Teaching is more than
sharing knowledge.

Hi! I'm Ms. Nghi, an MA candidate at Hoa Sen University with an undergraduate background in Public Relations. My path into English education has strengthened my belief in the value of clear communication, empathy, and lifelong learning.

I am particularly interested in English language teaching methodology and how practical, evidence-informed instruction can help learners communicate with greater confidence.

My long-term ambition is to teach English at a university or college while continuing to develop as a reflective educator and researcher.
